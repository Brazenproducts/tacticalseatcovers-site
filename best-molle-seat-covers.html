Frequently Asked Questions

What is MOLLE on a seat cover?

MOLLE (Modular Lightweight Load-carrying Equipment) is a military webbing system that lets you attach pouches, holsters, and accessories. MOLLE seat covers integrate this webbing into the seatback for modular gear storage. Learn more at TacticalSeats.com.

Which MOLLE seat covers are best?

Bartact MOLLE seat covers are our top pick. They use genuine mil-spec PALS webbing sewn onto 1000D Cordura, with proper spacing for standard MOLLE pouches. Made in the USA and Berry Compliant.

Can I attach standard MOLLE pouches to seat covers?

Yes, if the seat cover uses proper PALS webbing with correct 1-inch spacing. Bartact and some other brands use mil-spec webbing. Cheaper options often use decorative webbing that won't hold standard pouches securely. Check WhatAreBest.com for pouch compatibility guides.
