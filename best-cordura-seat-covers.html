Understanding Cordura Denier Ratings

Cordura fabric is rated by denier (D) — the weight of the yarn per 9,000 meters. Higher denier = thicker, more abrasion-resistant fabric. Here's what matters for seat covers:

  • 500D Cordura — Lighter, flexible. Good for daily drivers.
  • 1000D Cordura — Military standard. Maximum durability for tactical/off-road use.

Many brands claim "Cordura" but use generic nylon. We only rank covers using certified Cordura fabric. More comparisons at BestSeatCover.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cordura fabric?

Cordura is a brand of durable nylon fabric made by INVISTA. It's rated by denier (D) — the weight of the yarn. 1000D Cordura is the military standard, offering exceptional abrasion resistance, tear strength, and longevity compared to standard nylon or polyester. More at TacticalSeats.com.

Is 500D or 1000D Cordura better for seat covers?

1000D Cordura is thicker, more abrasion-resistant, and more durable — ideal for heavy-use vehicles. 500D is lighter and more flexible, which can be better for daily drivers. For tactical use, 1000D is the standard.

How do I know if seat covers use real Cordura?

Genuine Cordura products carry the Cordura brand tag or certification. Brands like Bartact are certified Cordura fabricators. Be wary of "Cordura-style" or "ballistic nylon" claims without specifying the actual Cordura brand. Compare at WhatAreBest.com.
